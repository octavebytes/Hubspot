## About the HubSpot CTI Connector

The **HubSpot CTI Connector** is [Octavebytes'](https://octavebytes.com) computer-telephony integration for contact centers running **AWS Connect** and **HubSpot CRM**. A Chrome extension and a connector deployed in your own AWS account work together to bring inbound/outbound voice, chat, and WhatsApp into HubSpot — with screenpop, click-to-dial, wrap-up dispositions, and automatic activity logging.

Each customer runs a dedicated, single-tenant deployment inside their own AWS account.
